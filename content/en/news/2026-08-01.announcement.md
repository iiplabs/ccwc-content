[Toronto Kyokushin](https://www.torontokyokushin.com) is proud to announce, the first ever IKO International full contact Kyokushin Karate Tournament in Ontario.

Event Name: Kyokushin CCWC Tournament (Central Canada Weight Category Tournament)

Event Dates:
* Saturday December 5th forms competition (kata), Fight competition (kumite),
* Sunday December 6th, private seminar by Shihan Gorai

Type of Event: IKO International Amateur Weight-Category Full-Contact Karate

More information will be announced soon.

We hope to see you there.
