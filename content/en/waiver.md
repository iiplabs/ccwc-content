**Junior and Senior Kumite:**
Juniors under 18 and Seniors 40+ will always be divided by weight categories even if held in conjunction with Open Category Adult tournament. Their weight categories will be based on further division by age as well as can also be by rank based on entries received for the championships. Some categories in the Junior and Senior divisions may not receive enough entries to warrant inclusion to the tournament. Junior and senior category entry come with no guarantee. If there are not appropriate entries to make a suitable category, those that do not have suitable opponents to compete with will be given a full refund of their entry fees.

IKO-Kyokushinkai Karate is an amateur combative sport. Weight cutting is discouraged at all times. Competitors should enter and compete in their natural weight category. The use of plastic bags, diuretics, rubber suits or any artificial methods to induce rapid weight loss are prohibited.

For weight in the day prior to competition, any adult competitor that exceed their allotted weight at weigh in shall be given until the end of the check in that day to make their weight. In this instance the same day weight loss cannot exceed 2.5% of their weight as shown on the weigh in. 

For weight in the day prior to competition, any youth competitor that exceed their allotted weight at weigh shall be given until the end of the check in that day to make their weight. In this instance the same day weight loss cannot exceed 1% of their weight as shown on the weigh in. 

Competitors that are unable to meet weight, or are found to have contravened the weight loss rules by using prohibited artificial weight loss methods, shall be disqualified and not eligible for any refund or reimbursements of their entry fee or any costs to participate. 

Competitors with missing medical clearance by the end of the designated check in period shall be disqualified and not eligible for any refund or reimbursements of their entry fee or any costs to participate.
